Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Marginal ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, provided when you create an account.
  • Notes content: the titles, body text, and categories of notes you create in the app.
  • Camera access: if you use the Scan feature, the app accesses your camera to capture a photo for text recognition. This happens on-device (or via Google Play Services' on-device model) — the photo itself is not uploaded to our servers or stored after the text is extracted. Only the recognized text becomes part of your note, which you control like any other note content.

How we use this information

Your account and notes data is used solely to provide the app's core functionality: authenticating you, syncing your notes across your devices, and letting you search and organize them. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Marginal is built on the following third-party services, each of which processes data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — manages account sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) — stores your notes data.
  • Google ML Kit / Google Play Services — powers on-device text recognition for the Scan feature.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how these services handle data.

Data security

Your notes are stored per-account, and our database security rules ensure only you can read or write your own notes — no other user of the app can access your data.

Data retention

Your notes and account information are retained until you delete individual notes, or delete your account entirely (see below).

Children's privacy

Marginal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Delete My Account

You can delete your Marginal account and all associated notes in either of these ways:

Option 1 — In the app

Open Marginal → Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete account → confirm with your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and every note in it.

Option 2 — Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email muhammadzahib99@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my Marginal account." We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes: your account credentials, your name and email, and every note you've created. This action cannot be undone.
