Delete Your Marginal Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Marginal account and data — with or without the app installed.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (email, name)
  • Every note you've created — titles, content, and categories

This action cannot be undone. There is no recovery period once it's processed.

Option 1 — Fastest: delete it in the app

If you still have Marginal installed, this is instant:

Open Marginal → Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete account → confirm with your password. Your account and all notes are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, use the form below. It opens your email client with a pre-filled request — just hit send.

This opens your default email app addressed to our support address, with your details filled in. Send it from the same email address associated with your account so we can verify the request.

What happens next

Once we receive your request by email, we will delete your account and all associated notes within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.
